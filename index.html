Theory

Newton's law of cooling states that the rate of loss of heat of a body is directly proportional to the difference in temperatures of the body and its surroundings, provided the temperature difference is small.

-dT / dt = k (T - Ts)

On integrating, T(t) = Ts + (T0 - Ts) * e^(-kt)

  • T = Temperature of the body at time t
  • T0 = Initial temperature of the body
  • Ts = Temperature of the surrounding medium
  • k = Positive constant depending upon the area and nature of the surface of the body

Initially, cooling is rapid due to a high thermal gradient. As the body cools towards room temperature, the rate of cooling decreases exponentially.
